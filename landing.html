FirstVisit AI: Smart Customer Retention System

FirstVisit AI: Smart Customer Retention System

Many first-time customers visit Great Clips once but do not return. This team designed an AI-powered system that personalizes the first visit and follows up afterward to turn one-time customers into repeat clients.

The Problem

First-time customers often feel unfamiliar with the service and do not build a strong connection, leading to low return rates.

The Solution

The system uses a short intake and AI to personalize the visit, support the stylist, and automatically follow up with the customer after their haircut.

Why It Works

It creates a better first experience, builds trust, and encourages customers to return without requiring additional marketing spend.

Check-in → AI → Personalized Visit → Follow-up → Return Visit

AI Loyalty & Smart Recommendations System

Many restaurant customers visit once but do not return regularly. This team designed an AI-powered system that personalizes menu recommendations and rewards to increase repeat visits and customer loyalty.

Customer Order → AI Preferences → Menu Recommendation → Reward → Return Visit

Google Sheets — Data Tracking, Budget Management, and Operational Control

Google Sheets served as the central data infrastructure for the entire program, allowing us to track progress, manage resources, and maintain full visibility across all aspects of execution.

How We Used It

  • built a master tracking system for all sessions, including pacing, completion status, and alignment with planned milestones
  • developed a full budget sheet comparing projected vs. actual costs across all categories
  • tracked sponsor contributions, allocation of funds, and real-time financial status
  • organized student participation data, including attendance, engagement levels, and completion rates
  • monitored key program metrics in a structured, continuously updated format

Project Management Impact

  • ensured real-time visibility into program progress and performance
  • enabled precise financial control and cost optimization decisions
  • supported data-driven adjustments by identifying gaps and inefficiencies
  • improved accountability by centralizing all critical project data
  • allowed the team to manage both operations and finances within a single system
